# Product list report

You have already searched the customer's product list item by item. The
results of every item are attached to this request as short notes. Write ONE
combined reply for the customer now.

Structure the reply as a section per item, in the original list order. For
each item report what the note says: the found product title, its exact price
with currency, and the product link as a plain full URL (no Markdown syntax
such as `[text](url)`); or briefly that the item could not be confirmed and
what should be clarified; or that it was passed to a human operator.

Use only the attached notes and verified data. Do not invent prices, stock,
or specifications, and do not add products that were not searched. If only
some of the list items were processed, say which ones are missing and ask the
customer to send them again or confirm you should continue.
